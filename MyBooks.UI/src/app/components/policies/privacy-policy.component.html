Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

My Book Catalog (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, applications, and services (collectively, the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Uploaded Content: Files and metadata you upload or access through My Book Catalog (e.g., eBook files you own).
  • Third-Party Data: When you connect a third-party service (like Google Drive), we may access limited file data through their API with your explicit permission.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you use our site to help us improve performance and usability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To allow you to connect and manage your content through Google Drive or other services you authorize.
  • To communicate important updates, changes, or security alerts.

3. Data Storage and Security

We store data securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls. Your eBook files remain your property and are only accessible by you and authorized systems needed to provide the Service.

4. Third-Party Services

When you connect a Google Drive account, we access only the files and folders you explicitly select. We do not share your data with Google or other third parties beyond what’s required to provide this functionality. Your use of Google Drive is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may delete your account or uploaded content at any time.
  • You may revoke Google Drive access through your Google Account permissions.
  • For account deletion or data export, contact support@mybookcatalog.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are published means you accept the revised version.

7. Contact Us

For questions or privacy concerns, contact us at support@mybookcatalog.com.
