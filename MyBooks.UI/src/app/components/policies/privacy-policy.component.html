Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

My Book Catalog (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, applications, and services (collectively, the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Uploaded Content: Files and metadata you upload or access through My Book Catalog (e.g., eBook files you own).
  • Third-Party Data: When you connect a third-party storage or authentication service (such as Google Drive or OneDrive), we may access limited file metadata and identifiers through their API with your explicit consent. We do not access or store your files unless you specifically select them for use within the Service.
  • Technical Data: We may automatically collect limited technical information (such as IP address, device type, and browser version) for security, fraud prevention, and system optimization purposes.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you use our site to help us improve performance and usability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Service and comply with legal obligations.
  • To allow you to connect and manage your content through third-party storage accounts or other connected services you authorize.
  • To communicate important updates, changes, or security alerts.
  • To send transactional messages such as account verification, password resets, or policy updates.

3. Data Storage and Security

We store data securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls. Your files remain your property and are only accessible by you and authorized systems needed to provide the Service. If cloud storage providers are used, all files are stored in accordance with applicable data protection laws and are logically isolated by account to ensure privacy and security.

4. Third-Party Services

When you connect a third-party storage account, we access only the files or folders you explicitly select. We do not share your data with third parties beyond what is required to provide this functionality or comply with applicable laws and regulations. Your use of third-party storage is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may delete your account or uploaded content at any time.
  • You may revoke third-party storage account access through that third-party account's permission settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are published means you accept the revised version.

7. Contact Us
